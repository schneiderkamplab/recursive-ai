# Research TODO

- [ ] Investigate **linguistic accommodation and lexical alignment** in
  consumer–AI interaction.
  - Examine both directions: AI accommodation to the consumer's vocabulary,
    syntax, register, tone, and framing; and, especially, the consumer's
    subsequent accommodation to language introduced by the AI.
  - Treat communication accommodation, lexical entrainment, language-style
    matching, and conceptual or framing alignment as distinguishable candidate
    mechanisms rather than interchangeable labels.
  - Test whether consumers reuse AI-introduced words, metaphors, categories,
    identity labels, evaluative terms, and causal formulations in later turns.
  - Distinguish superficial stylistic mimicry from substantive uptake. Repeated
    terminology is evidence of recursive extension only when it helps represent,
    interpret, or organize the self, an identity-bearing project, or a
    consequential choice.
  - Operationalize direction and sequence at the turn level: user baseline → AI
    accommodation/transformation → user lexical or conceptual uptake → later
    recursive reuse.
  - Combine qualitative tracing with quantitative measures of asymmetric
    lexical reuse, semantic alignment, style matching, and persistence.
  - Add prompt fields or post-processing measures only in a new measurement
    version; do not alter the v5 ontology or historical results silently.
