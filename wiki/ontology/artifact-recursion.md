# Definition

Artifact recursion occurs whenever user and AI iteratively transform a document, codebase, fictional world, design, brand, campaign, or other artifact.

# Relation to extension levels

Artifact recursion may coexist with L2 project/possession extension or L3 representational extension. At L0, however, it is a critical negative contrast: the artifact evolves, but the consumer's evidenced self or identity-bearing project does not.

# Diagnostic questions

1. What exactly recurs: a task artifact, an owned project, a self-representation, or a self-understanding?
2. Is the artifact explicitly or compellingly linked to the user?
3. Does the user respond to how the AI represents the user, or only to output quality?
4. Does an AI-produced self/project formulation become a premise for a later decision?

# Canonical negative

The fictional sitcom conversation repeatedly elaborates a fictional character and story world. It is rich artifact recursion but L0 and `none`, because the transcript does not establish the character as the user's self. See [hard negatives](/evidence/hard-negatives.md).
