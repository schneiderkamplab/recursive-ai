# Status

L6 is an **outlook concept for future developments**, not part of the v5 ontology, prompt, schema, automated audit, or current manual coding. Current records must remain coded L0-L5. Operationalizing L6 would create a new measurement version and require separate calibration and output files.

# Provisional definition

**L6 delegated/agentic extension** occurs when a consumer authorizes a persistent AI system to represent, decide, negotiate, transact, coordinate, or otherwise act across contexts on the consumer's behalf, and consequential outcomes recursively feed back into the consumer's choices, relationships, identity, or self-understanding.

At L6, an AI-mediated possible self is not merely represented, interpreted, or enacted through consumer decisions. Elements of that self become operationalized through delegated, persistent AI agency.

# Boundary with L5

| L5 enacted extension | Prospective L6 agentic extension |
|---|---|
| AI configuration organizes the consumer's intended choices or actions. | The consumer delegates bounded authority for the AI to act or represent the consumer. |
| The consumer remains the principal enactor within the observed process. | The AI performs consequential operations across time, systems, or institutions. |
| A conversation can evidence intended enactment. | Evidence must include persistent delegation and externally consequential AI action or representation. |
| The feedback loop concerns how AI-shaped understanding informs action. | Outcomes of AI action return to reshape subsequent delegation, action, or self-understanding. |

# Provisional evidentiary requirements

A future L6 classification should require direct evidence of all of the following:

1. An actual-user identity, relationship, value, goal, or identity-bearing project anchor.
2. A qualifying recursive process at L3-L5.
3. Explicit or behaviorally demonstrated delegation of authority beyond producing a conversational answer.
4. Persistence across time, tasks, platforms, or institutional encounters.
5. Consequential AI action or representation, such as negotiating, transacting, communicating, scheduling, purchasing, allocating, or coordinating for the consumer.
6. Recursive feedback in which outcomes alter later instructions, choices, boundaries, identity claims, or self-understanding.

# Exclusions

The following would not by themselves support L6:

* asking AI what to buy or do;
* intending to follow AI advice;
* ordinary tool use or a one-off API call;
* fictional agent role-play;
* scheduled prompts without delegated consequential authority;
* persistent memory without action;
* autonomous-looking output when the transcript does not evidence actual authorization or external effect.

# Illustrative future phenomena

Potential empirical settings include AI systems that:

* negotiate recurring purchases, subscriptions, insurance, credit, or service disputes;
* maintain a consumer's market-facing profile and communicate with institutions;
* coordinate housing, travel, health-consumption, education, or household arrangements over time;
* operate a venture or creative marketplace presence within delegated boundaries;
* learn from transaction or interaction outcomes and propose revised identity-relevant goals;
* represent different facets of the consumer across platforms while the consumer negotiates consistency, privacy, and control.

# Research implications

L6 would shift analysis from AI-mediated self-representation and choice toward **delegated self-operation**. Relevant theoretical questions include ownership of AI action, continuity of identity across systems, consumer control and revocation, accountability for misrepresentation, institutional recognition of AI agents, and how feedback from delegated action changes the consumer.

The existing public chat corpora are unlikely to provide sufficient verification because they primarily record conversation rather than external agent action. Future research would benefit from longitudinal agent logs, transaction or communication traces, interviews, diaries, and observation of consumer-agent-institution interactions.

# Versioning requirement

If L6 is operationalized:

1. Create a new prompt and schema version rather than editing v5 in place.
2. Define what counts as authorization, persistence, consequence, and feedback.
3. Calibrate against L5 cases, simulated-agent hard negatives, and verified agent-action cases.
4. Use new append-only output and error files.
5. Decide whether historical conversations are incomparable or warrant a targeted rerun.
