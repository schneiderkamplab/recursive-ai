# Methodological Decisions

* [Broad consumer scope](broad-consumer-scope.md) - Context is tagged rather than used as an exclusion gate.
* [Levels and recursive labels](levels-versus-labels.md) - Level and evidential completeness are separate dimensions.
* [Recall-sensitive potentials](recall-sensitive-potentials.md) - False positives are preferred to false negatives during candidate retrieval.
* [Clear-case threshold](clear-case-threshold.md) - Only L3–L5 with a complete ordered chain qualify as clear.
* [Consumer orientation without a professional-self construct](consumer-not-professional-self.md) - Occupational material may qualify, but the study does not name a professional-self category.
