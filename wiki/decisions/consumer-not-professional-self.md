# Decision

Do not call the focal construct “professional self.” Occupational and entrepreneurial contexts remain eligible under the same L0–L5 ontology when consumer use, an owned identity-bearing project, self-representation, reflexivity, or enactment is evidenced.

# Rationale

Excluding work-related consumption would be artificially narrow, but centering a professional-self construct would shift the project away from its intended wider consumer orientation.

# Consequence

Use context tags such as `occupational` or `entrepreneurial`; name the extension locus as capability, project/possession, representation, reflexivity, or enactment.
