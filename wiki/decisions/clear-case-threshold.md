# Decision

Only representational, reflexive, and enacted extension—L3 through L5—can receive `clear`, and only when all four stages are directly and chronologically evidenced.

# Rationale

This makes “recursive AI-extended self” more demanding than iterative AI assistance or co-creation. It also prevents unrelated later corrections from being recruited to complete an earlier chain, as occurred in the automated assessment of the age-inference case.

# Consequence

Strong L2 cases remain `potential` by design. They are not weak interactions; they instantiate a different relationship between AI, project/possession, and self.
