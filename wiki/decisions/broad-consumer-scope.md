# Decision

Consumers include users consuming AI, education, advice, creative systems, marketplace knowledge, and other resources. Work, study, or entrepreneurship does not itself exclude a conversation.

# Rationale

Narrowly equating consumption with household purchase would discard theoretically relevant AI use, learning, prosumption, and marketplace practice. The wider scope better matches consumer-culture and JCR traditions.

# Guardrail

Mere AI use remains insufficient for self-extension. Consumer relevance is descriptive and never gates the extension label. See [consumer scope](/ontology/consumer-scope.md).
